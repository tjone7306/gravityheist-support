Gravity Heist — Support

Gravity Heist is a one-burn slingshot puzzle. Aim, release, and let gravity bend the line into the goal.

How to play

  • Drag from the ship to aim. Release to launch. You get one burn.
  • Closer passes bend harder. A wide pass is a nudge.
  • The camera chip switches ride, chase, and overview. A tap switches. A drag aims.
  • Hold during flight to fast-forward a shot you already trust.
  • MENU leaves the level without scoring it.

Progress

Stars, scores, and the ship and camera you picked stay on the phone. There is no account to recover them on another device. Deleting the app clears them.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue (a free GitHub account is needed). Include the device model and what you expected the shot to do.

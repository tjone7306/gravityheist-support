Privacy Policy

Gravity Heist, by Tim Jones. Bundle identifier com.tjones.gravityheist. This policy covers version 1.0.

Data we collect

None. Gravity Heist does not collect personal data, does not track you, and does not send gameplay, diagnostics, or identifiers to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

The game stores your progress, the ship you picked, the flight camera you picked, and the music and sound settings in the app's own storage on the device. That information is not transmitted. Deleting the app deletes it.

Accounts and purchases

There is no account, no sign-in, and no advertising. The game does not use Game Center.

Motion and sensors

The game does not read the motion sensors.

Children

The game does not collect data from anyone, including children.
